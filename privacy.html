Privacy Policy – Apna Cash Khata

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time login link.
  • Ledger data you enter: business names, customer names, transactions, amounts, notes and bank statement rows.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

How it is stored

Your data is stored securely with our hosting provider, Supabase. Access rules ensure that a business's data can be seen only by the members of that business.

How it is used

Your data is used only to provide the app's features. We never sell it, share it with advertisers or use it for marketing.

Your choices

  • You can delete customers and entries in the app.
  • To delete your account and all of its data, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.
