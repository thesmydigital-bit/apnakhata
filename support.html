Common questions

How do I sign in?
Enter your email address and tap the login link we send you. No password is needed.

How do I share my ledger with a partner?
Add them as a member of your business. They sign in with their own email and see the same ledger.

How do I back up my data?
Use the export/backup option in the app to save a full copy to Files, Drive, email or WhatsApp.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to the "Choose business" screen and tap Delete account at the bottom. You can also email us from the address you sign in with.
