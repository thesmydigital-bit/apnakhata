2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Apna Cash Khata is a digital "khata" (credit ledger) for shopkeepers and small business owners, mainly in Pakistan, India and the UK. Small shops traditionally record customer credit ("udhaar") and supplier payments in paper registers, which get lost, are hard to total, and can't be shared with partners. The app lets the owner record money given and received per customer or supplier, see running balances, attach bill photos, share one ledger with partners or staff, reconcile against a bank statement, view reports, and export a backup. It is free and intended for the general public.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
- Demo account: on the login screen, type saudmyousaf+applereview@gmail.com. A password field appears. Enter the password provided in the Sign-In Information section and tap "Sign in". (Regular users sign in with a one-time code emailed to them. Signing up is the same flow.)
- After signing in, choose the sample business "Ali General Store" to see example customers and transactions.
- Add an entry: open a customer, enter an amount, choose "gave" or "got", optionally attach a bill photo, and save.
- "Reports" shows totals. "Reconcile bank" matches bank statement rows against entries.
- "Back up all data" (on the Choose business screen) exports a JSON backup through the share sheet.
- Delete account: on the "Choose business" screen, tap "Delete account" at the bottom, then confirm.
No sample files are required.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (supabase.com): database, authentication (email one-time code), and file storage for bill photos.
No payment processors, analytics, advertising or AI services are used.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. The user picks a display currency (INR, USD, GBP or PKR) for each business; this only changes the currency symbol shown.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is a record-keeping tool only. It doesn't move money, provide loans or credit, connect to bank accounts, or process payments, and it contains no protected third-party material.
